Niche interests: five pieces to design

SPEC §3.9 (M8). Everything is built from what Tangent already has: the tile picker, the typographic card, mono chips, Logbook rows. There's no new colour, font or icon family. Phones 1, 2 and 4 are clickable. Each piece has a decision with my recommendation picked; change any of them, then press Send at the bottom.

01

Settings → Interests: the tree

Try it: tap Philosophy, History, Science or Maths (the ⑂ tiles) to open their tree. Tap a card header for a whole subfield, a chip for one topic; ‹ goes back.

Grid. Tiles with a tree carry a small branch mark (⑂) top-right while unpicked. Picked broadly: filled with a ✓ as today. Narrowed: filled, the ✓ becomes a count of picks below it, and a NARROWED line sits under the label. The counter keeps counting tiles touched, so the rule you see is the rule that's checked.

Tree screen, one page per tree tile, with no third level:

  • Header: the tile's territory block (same as a typographic card), with its icon, a live status (ALL OF PHILOSOPHY / NARROWED TO 2 CORNERS / NOT PICKED) and the All of Philosophy pill as the broad pick.
  • Route spine: the breadcrumb's dotted route line runs down from the header. Each subfield hangs off it on a pin that is hollow when unpicked, half-filled when some of its topics are picked, and solid when the whole subfield is picked. It's the same map idea as the Logbook route strip.
  • Subfield cards: card surface and lift, Fraunces title, and three sample articles in italics so you know what's inside (every card teaches). Tap the header to pick the whole subfield: the card tilts slightly and gets a territory outline, like a picked tile.
  • Topics as chips inside each card, with the approximate pool size (e.g. STOICISM 37). That sets expectations for running dry and lets you see the whole tree on one scroll. When the whole subfield is picked, its chips show dashed in territory colour (included); tapping one narrows the subfield to that topic.

Most specific wins, live on screen: ticking Logic unticks All of Philosophy; ticking Paradoxes turns Logic partial; clearing everything leaves the tile unpicked (not broad). Tick All again to make it broad. Nothing saves until Save on the grid, as today.

Tree contents are placeholders. The real nodes get curated and checked live in the build.

02

Home prompt card: Narrow Philosophy?

One non-article card type (shared with 03): the typographic card layout with the territory block, so it sits naturally in the column, and the block uses colour exactly as §2.4 allows. The title lives in the block, the body is one Literata sentence, and the chips are the tile's subfields.

  • Chip tap adds that pick straight away: the chip goes ink-filled with ✓, a toast says Added Logic · Home is rebuilding, light haptic, then Home rebuilds as after Save. How many chips one rebuild covers is the decision below.
  • Card body tap or SEE THE WHOLE TREE → opens Settings → Interests → Philosophy.
  • Swipe right dismisses (the card slides off right instead of rubber-banding). Left swipe does nothing: there's no article to take a tangent from. No first-hop chip on this card.
  • Why-line: → SWIPE RIGHT TO SKIP. No ✦, no ◌ ✓ badges.
When does a chip tap take effect?
Multi-select, rebuild after a 1.2 s pause RECOMMENDED
Someone into Ethics and Logic can tap both, and Home rebuilds once. The toast names every pick.
First tap rebuilds now (as SPEC)
Simplest. A second subfield means going through the tree.
03

Exhaustion card: You've read all of Stoicism

Same card type. The block gets a completion seal, drawn with the stamp template's double ring (§5.7) in bg.card ink and showing 37/37, so it reads as an achievement without a new art style. It uses the stamp toast's motion (scale 1.4→1.0, ink-spread, medium haptic) the first time the card comes into focus.

  • The body says what happens next: Home widens to Ethics from here. That's the leaf → subfield fallback, so it never feels like Home silently changed.
  • Chips = sibling nodes not already picked. They behave like the prompt card's chips. Swipe right dismisses.
  • Why-line: ✓ COMPLETED · PHILOSOPHY › ETHICS.
  • Shown once per node (when it's first written to completed_leaves).
04

Logbook: Completed · 2

Below Finds and above the expeditions list. Hidden while empty. Each row: a 40 pt territory swatch with the tile's icon (same as a Find without a thumbnail), node label in Fraunces 18, then a mono caption: path, article count, date. A ✓ at the right end.

Newest first. No cap and no See all, because it grows by at most a few per month.

05

Tappable topic label

Only on cards whose tile has a tree. Pressed: a line.hairline pill fills behind ● PHILOSOPHY (120 ms in, 200 ms out), with hitSlop up to 44 pt tall. Release opens the tile's tree. The label colour doesn't change. Press and hold the labels on the left to see it.

The card's own tap (open reader) still covers the rest of the card. The label wins only inside its pill.
